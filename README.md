<h2><strong>Free vs Paid: What to Expect</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Free courses from HubSpot, Google, Microsoft, Ahrefs, and Semrush teach the basics and give you a badge.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Paid programs add structure, projects, and, in some cases, a university or brand name.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A good plan is to start free, then pay only if you need a project to show an employer or a deeper skill.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Remember that a certificate alone rarely lands a job. A portfolio with real results counts more.</span></p>
<h2><strong>Follow the Rules When You Promote</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As you practice, learn the disclosure rules. The FTC says to disclose any financial, employment, personal, or family relationship with a brand, including free products, and to put the disclosure where it's hard to miss (FTC Disclosures 101, last modified December 3, 2024).&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Its consumer reviews and testimonials rule, effective October 21, 2024, bars fake reviews and undisclosed insider reviews.</span></p>

<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Weeks 7 to 9:</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Run a small project, such as a blog post, an email series, or a $50 ad test.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Weeks 10 to 12:</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Add analytics, record your results, and write a one-page case study.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Then decide.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Pay for a longer program if you want a credential or a career change.</span></li>
</ol>
<h2><strong>Common Mistakes to Avoid</strong></h2>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Collecting certificates without practice.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Apply each lesson to a real project.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Learning every channel at once.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Pick one first.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Paying before you try free options.</strong></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Trusting unsigned income claims.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Check any earnings figure at its source.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Ignoring provider dates.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Tools and rules change, so confirm that a course is current.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Skipping analytics.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> You can't improve what you don't measure.</span></li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>Frequently Asked Questions</strong></h2>
<h3><strong>What is the best digital marketing course for beginners?</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Google Digital Marketing &amp; E-commerce Professional Certificate and the University of Illinois specialization on Coursera are two broad options. HubSpot's free Digital Marketing Certification is a faster start.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Are free digital marketing courses worth it?</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Yes, for basics. HubSpot, Google, Microsoft, Ahrefs, and Semrush list free courses. Paid programs add structure and projects.</span></p>
<h3><strong>How long does it take to learn digital marketing?</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Coursera lists about 6 months at 10 hours a week for Google's certificate and about 3 months at 10 hours a week for the Illinois specialization. Short free courses take a few hours.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Do I need a degree to start?</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Coursera lists the Google and Illinois programs as beginner level with no prior experience required.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Which certification is best for ads?</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising both offer free training. Choose the platform you'll use.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Which course is best for SEO?</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ahrefs' SEO Course for Beginners and Yoast's free courses are two low-cost starting points. Semrush Academy also lists SEO courses.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Will a certificate get me a job?</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Not by itself. Pair it with a portfolio of real work.</span></p>
<h3><strong>How much do digital marketing courses cost?</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Many are free. Coursera lists $49 a month for Google's certificate, and Yoast Premium costs $49 a month or $89 a year.</span></p>
<h2><strong>The Bottom Line</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Start with one broad course, such as Google's certificate on Coursera or HubSpot's free certification, then go deep on one channel and build a small project.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Use free options first, pay for structure only when you need it, and confirm every price and date on the provider's site before you enroll. Skills turn into income when you show results, not just certificates.</span></p>
